    ❓ How to Fish

    🖱️

    Click the water to cast. When the bobber plunges, click fast to hook.

    ⬆️

    Hold left mouse to raise the reel bar, release to sink. Keep the fish inside — the gauge fills while covered, drains while not.

    ⚡

    Superpower: keeping the fish in your bar charges the round gauge at the bottom. When full, click it, right-click, or press F. Every character has a unique power.

    🔥

    Combo: consecutive PERFECT catches multiply your ⭐ score up to ×3. Fast rounds earn 🥇🥈🥉 medals. Try the Daily Catch — same seeded waters for everyone, leaderboard glory.

    ⚡

    After every catch the tide offers 3 blessings, Common → Legendary. Build your run.

    🪱

    An escaped fish costs 1 worm. Run out and the run ends. Survive all 10 waters and land the Leviathan.
